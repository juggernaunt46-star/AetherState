# Post-1.24 Engineering Hardening Design

**Date:** 2026-07-24

**Status:** Approved design, pending written-spec review

**Baseline:** Public AetherState 1.24.0 at `82b58277d7a1fb167434be0290d3dfd2bb3588e2`

## 1. Purpose

Run one bounded post-1.24 engineering-hardening project that makes AetherState safer to update,
repair, extend, and maintain without turning the work into a rewrite or a disguised feature cycle.
The project is delivered in independently useful stages. Each completed stage must leave AetherState
working, testable, and suitable for a release candidate even if later stages have not begun.

The project addresses the specific structural risks established in the current code:

- central responsibility and change coupling in `state.py`, `pipeline.py`, `control.py`, and
  `store.py`;
- string-discriminated dictionary contracts at high-authority operation boundaries;
- broad fail-open exception handling without one truthful degradation surface;
- hand-managed SQLite schema evolution;
- CI that proves the main runtime well but does not continuously prove Windows, built-package,
  historical-upgrade, or typed-boundary behavior.

It preserves AetherState's existing engineering strengths: deterministic code-owned mechanics,
validated and journaled mutation, replay compatibility, exact stream relay, privacy, credential
compartmentalization, Chat/RPG separation, and transaction-owned durable truth.

## 2. Non-negotiable preservation rule

**No existing player-facing or functional AetherState behavior may be stripped by this project.**

That includes removal, hiding, weakening, silent replacement, advanced-only demotion, route loss,
renaming that breaks access, narrower supported content, or an internal equivalent that no longer
gives the Player the same usable result. A refactor that cannot preserve a current surface stops at
HOLD; the surface does not pay for code cleanliness.

The protected baseline is cumulative. It is the union of:

- public 1.24.0;
- the actual implementation or merge target when a stage begins;
- every behavior delivered by earlier completed hardening stages; and
- player-facing or functional behavior added to the target while this project is underway.

Stage 1 creates an append-only **Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest** from public
1.24.0 documentation, tests, synthetic live flows, routes, commands, launchers, background behavior,
error behavior, availability contracts, and extension controls. Before any stage merge or
publication candidate, refresh it against the current target and rerun its cumulative gates. Only a
separate explicitly approved product decision may authorize removing an entry.

At minimum the manifest covers:

- Windows and Linux installation, start, status, and stop paths;
- the transparent OpenAI-compatible proxy and existing fail-open/fail-closed behavior;
- the Console, Creator, card import/export/install, configuration, and control APIs;
- ordinary Chat, first-class Chat/Living Character, Character Core, Persona, Chat Continuity,
  swipe/fork/reopen behavior, and Chat Creator;
- RPG selection, Player Cards, RPG Creator, HUD, War Room, checks, combat, inventory, progression,
  world behavior, and existing mature-content capability;
- WorldLex, PlayerLex, Player Lessons, Claim Records, Facts, World Events, memory, retrieval,
  narration, and current inspection surfaces;
- every other behavior already proven functional in public tests or the installed 1.24.0 build.

The manifest is a minimum preservation inventory, not a loophole. Discovering an omitted working
surface or non-UI contract adds it to the manifest; omission or lack of an existing test never
authorizes removal. Default visibility and reachability are part of the behavior. Deprecation,
support narrowing, or product simplification requires a separate explicitly approved product
decision and is outside this project.

A proven defect is recorded with its failing contract and evidence; it is not protected as intended
behavior. Repair may correct that defect, but may not use it as a reason to remove the surrounding
working feature or access path.

## 3. Other hard invariants

1. Models may recognize, score, author proposals, or narrate; validated code continues to own
   mechanics, authority, truth, journaling, replay, and commits.
2. Existing public APIs, configuration keys, operation JSON, journal rows, database meaning, card
   formats, extension controls, supported operating systems/Python versions, and replay behavior
   remain compatible.
3. Non-gated traffic retains byte-transparent streaming and availability. Existing semantic
   fail-closed boundaries remain fail-closed.
4. No diagnostic surface may expose prompts, story text, personal paths, credentials, raw provider
   data, private lesson text, or raw exception messages.
5. The dirty personal checkout and its private history remain untouched by implementation work.
   Implementation uses an isolated worktree based on the verified public baseline or its explicitly
   approved successor.
6. File-size reduction is evidence, not the objective. Ownership, dependency direction, typed
   contracts, independent testing, and preserved behavior decide success.
7. No stage is published, released, or merged merely because it passes locally. Publication remains
   a separate authorized action.
8. Verification is proportional and time-budgeted. The project does not trade rigor for speed, but
   it also does not serialize unchanged gates, repeat the full suite after every repair, or consume
   hours proving unaffected surfaces. A projected or elapsed over-budget gate enters
   `TEST_BUDGET_HOLD` for sharding, test optimization, or scope correction before work continues.

## 4. Selected approach

Use **safety rails first, then bounded restructuring**.

Rejected alternatives:

- An architecture-first rewrite gives faster visual cleanup but puts database, replay, streaming,
  and player behavior at unnecessary risk.
- Opportunistic cleanup during unrelated feature work causes no clear finish line and leaves the
  most dangerous shared boundaries socially rather than mechanically protected.

The selected ordering is:

1. freeze behavior and proof;
2. execute the current-main Semantic Cube narrator-output integrity stage;
3. version schema evolution;
4. make degradation truthful and visible;
5. type high-authority operation admission;
6. reduce orchestration coupling behind those gates;
7. close the project with full cross-platform and compatibility proof.

## 5. Stage 1 — Safety baseline and preservation proof

### Work

- Establish the isolated implementation worktree and prove source/import origin.
- Record current module size, route count, dispatch count, broad-exception sites, dependency edges,
  and CI coverage as comparison data. These are diagnostics, not arbitrary pass thresholds.
- Create the Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest and map each entry to an automated
  contract, synthetic smoke, or explicit bounded manual acceptance check.
- Add architectural characterization tests around:
  - pure state reduction and live-versus-replay equivalence;
  - journal, receipt, retry, swipe, fork, reopen, and accepted-response identity;
  - pipeline request admission, response acceptance, and cold-path isolation;
  - exact route paths and response shapes;
  - exact non-gated proxy bytes, malformed SSE, upstream errors, and bounded tees;
  - current extension controls and every established HUD/Creator flow.
- Add CI jobs that build wheel and sdist artifacts, install the built artifact into a clean
  environment, start the service, verify status, and stop it cleanly.
- Add a Windows Python 3.12 CI lane. Retain Linux Python 3.10 and 3.12.
- Inventory every public database schema from the oldest version the current product claims to
  support through 1.24.0, record a deterministic schema fingerprint and source revision for each,
  and give every intervening release an explicit identical-schema, migrated, unsupported, or HOLD
  disposition. The inventory must explicitly resolve 1.21.0, 1.22.x if any public build carried a
  distinct or supported schema, 1.23.0, and 1.24.0. Create only synthetic fixtures; never read
  personal Player data. A supported schema that cannot be reconstructed is HOLD until Bean makes a
  separate support decision. `Unsupported` requires public evidence that the version never carried
  a supported upgrade contract; it cannot silently narrow support.
- Configure static checking only for new hardening modules and later migrated modules. Do not make
  an unbounded whole-repository annotation conversion a prerequisite.

### Exit gate

- Current 1.24.0 behavior is represented by the Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest.
- Characterization, package-build, clean-install, Linux, Windows, JavaScript, privacy, and full
  pytest gates pass.
- No runtime or player-facing behavior has changed.

## 6. Stage 2 — Current-main Semantic Cube narrator-output integrity

Stage 2 executes the approved **Current-Main Semantic Cube Narrator-Output Integrity Design**
unchanged as an independently completable integrity project. Its canonical contract is
`2026-07-24-semantic-cube-narrator-output-integrity-design.md` at design commit `bd4dee9`. That
document must be present beside this umbrella design in the implementation-planning worktree; this
section does not replace, summarize away, or weaken it.

### Fixed scope and ordering

- Audit exact current-main narrator-output behavior across recognition; binding/world alignment;
  admission/complete settlement; narrator transfer/model compliance; and visibility/lifecycle.
- Cover Exploration, Combat Opening, and Combat Exchange plus fresh response, duplicate transport,
  retry/lost reply, swipe/regeneration, Continue, reopen, branch, and replay.
- Use the Cube's first-failed-boundary rule, shared-invariant repair, current-main
  producer/consumer audit, and exact plus adversarial/lifecycle proof.
- Preserve the exact `PASS`, `HOLD`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, and `INVALID` status meanings.
- Keep the shipped default post-stream Narration Compliance Auditor distinct from the opt-in
  fail-closed semantic truth-gated path.
- Use automated synthetic evidence as the primary workload, focused disposable proof only for
  model/stream/UI claims, and one final mixed-mode discovery run.
- Run no runtime-changing hardening stage concurrently in the same Stage 2 candidate. Stage 1
  proof-only scaffolding may support the audit; Stages 3–6 wait for Stage 2's terminal report.

No LoRA, future narrator interpreter, personal-session inspection, new Cube runtime component,
phrase-patch campaign, feature invention, implicit publication, or release work enters Stage 2. A
Cube `HOLD` records a current-main limit or unresolved owner; it does not authorize a feature or
expand the hardening project.

### Exit gate and continuing role

Stage 2 exits only through the canonical semantic design's completion gate and delivers its complete
integrity matrix, bounded defect records, focused regressions, required disposable evidence, and
final `PASS/HOLD` report. Its full suite and complete-matrix run occur at the Stage 2 terminal gate,
not after every repair. Afterward:

- the complete matrix becomes a cross-cutting acceptance lane;
- each later hardening substage reruns every affected matrix cell;
- a change to a shared semantic, settlement, narrator-transfer, visibility, or lifecycle
  prerequisite invalidates and reruns dependent passes; and
- the final hardening gate reruns the complete matrix.

The matrix remains coordination and acceptance evidence. It never becomes runtime authority or a
System Health condition merely because a cell is `HOLD`.

## 7. Stage 3 — Versioned database evolution

### Architecture

Introduce one ordered migration registry and a durable migration ledger owned by Store's existing
SQLite connection and transaction lock. Each migration has:

- a stable integer version and name;
- an applicability predicate;
- one transactional schema/data transformation;
- postconditions;
- an idempotence check;
- a content-free failure code.

Existing schema creation remains available for a new database. Existing additive columns and bespoke
backfills are converted into a baseline version without reinterpreting already-upgraded databases.
No journal or state payload is rewritten unless a migration's exact postconditions require it.

### Required behavior

- A migration version is recorded only after schema changes, backfills, indexes, and postconditions
  succeed together.
- Startup after interruption either sees the old valid version or the fully completed new version.
- A second startup performs no additional mutation.
- Unsupported or corrupt states fail closed before destructive transformation and report a
  content-free diagnostic.
- Synthetic fixtures prove clean creation and every supported historical upgrade path.

### Exit gate

- Clean, historical, interrupted, repeated, and malformed migration cases pass.
- Existing 1.24.0 databases retain identical state, journal, branch, receipt, and replay meaning.
- Store remains the transaction authority.

## 8. Stage 4 — System Health and degradation truth

### Architecture

Add a small `SystemHealth` service with a bounded durable record written through the versioned
migration system. A health event contains only:

- subsystem;
- stable error code;
- expected-recoverable or unexpected-invariant classification;
- severity;
- correlation identifier;
- occurrence count;
- first and last timestamps;
- active or recovered state;
- successful recovery timestamp when applicable.

Conditions are keyed by `(subsystem, error_code, classification)`. The registry accepts only
declared subsystem/error-code pairs and retains one aggregate row per pair, so durability is bounded
by the registered condition catalog rather than time or traffic volume. A new occurrence reopens
the same recovered row and increments its count. A successful subsystem proof recovers only the
conditions it is defined to clear; unrelated success cannot erase another failure.

Raw exception messages, stack traces, prompts, generated text, credentials, personal paths, and
request bodies remain in neither the health database nor the UI.

### Error flow

```text
subsystem operation
    -> success: recover its active health condition when appropriate
    -> expected failure: preserve existing fail-open/fail-closed behavior + bounded health event
    -> unexpected failure: preserve the outer safety boundary + invariant health event + safe log
```

Broad catches remain only where they protect streaming, rollback, shutdown, optional cognition, or
another documented availability boundary. Redundant inner/outer catches are removed or narrowed.
An exception audit classifies every remaining broad catch rather than banning `except Exception`
mechanically. Ordinary logs retain only the error code, exception class, correlation identifier,
and content-free module/function/line frames. Exception messages, local variables, request data, and
unredacted personal paths require no ordinary logging path.

### Additive player-facing surface

- Replace the hardcoded status degradation value with truthful aggregate health.
- Add a compact Console System Health view showing safe subsystem status, recovery, counts, and
  correlation identifiers.
- Add a content-free diagnostic export for repair and support.

The existing `degradation` status key and string type remain stable: `none` means no active
condition, `degraded` means one or more active expected/recoverable conditions, and `failed` means
one or more active unexpected/invariant conditions. Unexpected/invariant takes precedence. Details
live under a new additive `health` object. The existing status route and Console control placement
remain available; the new view is additive and may not displace an existing control.

If durable health recording itself fails, the request/stream path keeps its existing behavior, the
condition remains in the bounded in-memory catalog for the process lifetime, and one rate-limited
content-free `health_recorder_unavailable` log code is emitted. The recorder never recursively
records its own failure.

### Exit gate

- Fault-injection tests prove expected failure, unexpected failure, recovery, bounded retention,
  privacy, and recorder-failure fallback.
- Proxy responses and semantic authority behavior remain unchanged under every injected fault.
- Console and status surfaces report truth without exposing content.

## 9. Stage 5 — Typed high-authority operation boundary

### Architecture

Persisted operation dictionaries and their JSON shapes remain compatible. Typing is introduced at
construction, admission, and dispatch:

```text
producer
    -> domain constructor
    -> typed validation result
    -> authority decision
    -> exact journal-compatible dictionary
    -> registered pure domain handler
    -> deterministic state
```

Use a frozen operation-definition registry plus domain-specific constructors and validated wrappers.
The registry owns operation kind, required fields, validator, authority class, handler owner, replay
compatibility policy, and schema/version identity. It does not create a second mechanics engine.

### Exact inventory entry gate

Before Stage 5 implementation, commit an exact operation-kind inventory derived from `_SPEC`,
`_CHAT_CONTINUITY_SPEC`, every fresh producer, every authority branch, and the synthetic legacy
replay fixtures. Each exact kind receives:

- one owning substage and domain;
- fresh-construction and replay status;
- current required fields and validator;
- authority sources;
- reducer/handler owner;
- persisted schema identity;
- affected Semantic Cube cells; and
- an explicit migrated-now or legacy-out-of-scope disposition.

The inventory, rather than the family labels below, is the normative scope boundary. An unlisted
kind cannot be silently absorbed into a substage.

### Independently passable substages

- **5A — Semantic commits:** meaning, binding, world alignment, and action-frame operation kinds.
- **5B — Settlement and combat evidence:** mechanic settlement, damage/effect evidence, and
  settlement-dependent combat admission kinds.
- **5C — Chat identity and continuity:** Chat Core and relationship/continuity record kinds.
- **5D — Claims and events:** Claim Record, Fact, World Event, and projection-authority kinds.
- **5E — WorldLex and Creator admission:** capability assignment and Creator admission kinds.

Each substage migrates only the exact kinds assigned to it and ends with its own independently green
proportional gate, cumulative Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest refresh, and
affected Semantic Cube matrix rerun. The full suite and complete Stage 5 cross-platform gate run
once after 5E, not after every family. A later substage does not need to be complete for an earlier
one to remain independently useful.

Legacy journals continue through one replay-only compatibility adapter. It is callable solely from
Store's trusted journal replay path after branch, journal-row, operation-index, and persisted
provenance validation. Live producers, APIs, models, extraction, Creator, user operations, and fresh
operations cannot select, mark, forge, or invoke legacy mode. A legacy-shaped operation without
trusted journal provenance rejects as a fresh malformed operation.

Unknown or malformed fresh operations cannot bypass typed admission. Every newly added operation
after Stage 5 begins must use the registry.

### Tests

- construction/serialization roundtrip;
- live-versus-replay equality;
- invalid-operation non-mutation;
- deterministic repeated reduction;
- registry completeness for the bounded families;
- exact legacy fixture replay;
- authority rejection and transaction rollback.

Generative/property testing may add a development-only dependency when it materially explores these
invariants; no coverage percentage substitutes for them.

### Stage exit gate

- All exact operation kinds assigned to 5A–5E use typed construction and registered ownership.
- Every substage passed independently and refreshed cumulative preservation and Cube evidence.
- Persisted JSON and replay behavior remain compatible.
- No model or caller gains a new mutation path.

## 10. Stage 6 — Bounded orchestration decomposition

This stage changes internal ownership without changing public behavior.

### 6A. Control

Split route registration by existing surfaces such as connection/configuration, sessions/HUD,
Creator/cards, PlayerLex/Player Lessons, Chat Core/continuity, and WorldLex. Keep one composition
router and preserve every URL, method, request, response, error status, and Console binding.

### 6B. Pipeline

Separate four explicit coordinators:

- request admission and experience/identity resolution;
- semantic selection and truth-gate lifecycle;
- response acceptance, delivery evidence, and swipe/retry lineage;
- optional cold-path extraction, memory, discovery, lint, and evolution scheduling.

They exchange typed context records and share no mutable hidden singleton state. Network or model
work never enters the pure state handler layer.

### 6C. State

Keep `state.py` as the visible reducer and authority composition root. Move the exact Stage 5
operation kinds into pure registered domain handlers. A handler receives state plus one validated
operation, performs no network/database work, and cannot import upward into the reducer orchestrator.

Do not split cohesive legacy reducer behavior merely to reduce line count. Further extraction is
allowed only when characterization tests prove a real ownership boundary.

### 6D. Store

Keep one connection, lock, transaction/savepoint owner, and replay spine. Move only versioned
migrations and clearly bounded repositories behind that owner. Do not create independent database
connections or cross-repository commit ambiguity.

Each of 6A–6D is independently passable and ends with an independently green proportional gate,
cumulative Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest refresh, and affected Semantic Cube
matrix rerun. The full suite and complete Stage 6 cross-platform gate run once after 6D, not after
every decomposition. The next decomposition does not begin from a failing predecessor.

### Exit gate

- Import-boundary tests enforce dependency direction.
- For every migrated family, validation and mutation no longer remain in orchestration modules
  except exact named replay or API compatibility shims covered by boundary tests.
- Each of 6A–6D passed independently.
- Route, pipeline, operation, journal, replay, database, UI, and player behavior parity pass.
- The Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest has no regression or unexplained removal.

## 11. Verification and delivery policy

Verification has three levels:

1. **Change loop:** exact failing regression, owning unit tests, one-variable/adversarial controls,
   and immediately affected lifecycle checks. Target at most 10 minutes of local elapsed time.
2. **Substage gate:** the change-loop proof plus the relevant broader boundary cohort, affected
   preservation-manifest entries, affected Semantic Cube cells, focused lint/static checks,
   compilation, and `git diff --check`. Target at most 20 minutes and enter `TEST_BUDGET_HOLD` at
   45 minutes of execution time.
3. **Major-stage/final gate:** the complete shared gate below. Independent operating-system,
   Python, package, and JavaScript jobs run in parallel. Stage 1 measures the actual baseline and
   records the declared budget; the default target is at most 45 minutes elapsed and the hard
   `TEST_BUDGET_HOLD` boundary is 90 minutes. The final project gate targets at most 60 minutes and
   also holds at 90 minutes of local serial execution without Bean's explicit approval. External CI
   queue time is reported separately; serially summing parallel
   runner-minutes does not redefine elapsed time.

The complete shared gate contains:

- focused unit and fault-injection tests;
- all characterization and Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest contracts;
- the complete required Semantic Cube matrix;
- full pytest;
- Ruff and static checking for declared modules;
- Python compilation;
- both JavaScript contracts and extension syntax;
- wheel/sdist build and clean install;
- Linux Python 3.10 and 3.12;
- Windows Python 3.12;
- synthetic historical database upgrades;
- exact proxy byte and streaming checks;
- import-origin and dirty-worktree isolation proof;
- privacy scan and content-free diagnostic inspection;
- `git diff --check`.

`TEST_BUDGET_HOLD` never authorizes skipping required proof. It requires test sharding,
parallelization, deterministic fixture reuse, removal of redundant duplicate coverage, test-speed
repair, or explicit scope/timing direction before the gate resumes. No blind live-turn quota is
allowed; live proof uses only the smallest scenario that distinguishes the relevant causes.

Passing evidence may be reused only for the exact same commit, environment contract, and unchanged
prerequisites. A later finding revokes and reruns dependent passes, not every unrelated green gate.
The same full suite is not rerun merely to rename a report or repackage unchanged evidence.

These CI lanes add proof; they do not narrow AetherState's currently declared operating-system or
Python support contract. Any support narrowing requires a separate explicitly approved product
decision.

Each stage receives its own reviewed commits and evidence record. A later stage may begin only from
the prior stage's passing baseline. If a finding invalidates an earlier prerequisite, dependent
passes are revoked and rerun. Every stage and substage refreshes the cumulative Behavior and Player
Surface Preservation Manifest and reruns its affected Semantic Cube cells before its exit gate.

## 12. Project completion gate

The bounded project is complete when all six stages and every named substage pass and:

- no existing player-facing or functional behavior has been stripped, hidden, weakened, or made
  harder to access;
- the cumulative Behavior and Player Surface Preservation Manifest matches the final target;
- the canonical Semantic Cube integrity design has a terminal report and its complete matrix passes
  the final cross-cutting acceptance run with only justified remaining `HOLD` or
  `NOT_APPLICABLE` cells;
- supported historical synthetic databases upgrade safely and repeatably;
- unexpected swallowed failures become observable without exposing content;
- every Stage 5 inventory kind uses its declared typed construction and registered handler;
- the selected control, pipeline, state, and Store boundaries have enforceable dependency direction;
- exact streaming, authority, replay, privacy, installation, and extension behavior remain intact;
- the repository and public documentation accurately describe the resulting ownership and repair
  surfaces.

No additional gameplay mechanic, content system, product redesign, deprecation, release ceremony,
or unrelated cleanup is part of this completion gate.
